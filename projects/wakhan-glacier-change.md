# Wakhan Glacier Change Analysis

## Overview

This project analyzes glacier change in the Wakhan Corridor of Badakhshan, Afghanistan, using GIS and Remote Sensing techniques.

## Study Period

2010–2024

## Objectives

- Analyze glacier area changes over time
- Detect glacier loss and new glacier formation
- Assess environmental and climate-related changes
- Produce GIS-based maps and spatial statistics

## Data & Methods

- Landsat 8 and Landsat 9
- Remote Sensing
- NDSI
- GIS spatial analysis
- ArcGIS Pro

## Outputs

The project includes glacier change maps, spatial analysis, area statistics, and environmental interpretation.

## Tools

- ArcGIS Pro
- Remote Sensing
- GIS
- Satellite Imagery
- NDSI
